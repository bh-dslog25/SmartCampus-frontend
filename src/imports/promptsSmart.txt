Create a production-oriented frontend for the **SmartCampus AI Agent** IoT operations system.

Use:

* React
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS
* ThreeJS

The frontend must be designed around the actual backend data contracts.

IMPORTANT:

* Do not invent backend fields.
* Do not invent AI response fields.
* Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought.
* Do not create fake operational metrics.
* Use mock data only where necessary for prototype visualization.
* Clearly distinguish IoT data, AI Agent responses, and operator actions.

Backend:

* Edge Gateway: `http://localhost:8000`
* AI Service: `http://localhost:8001`
* AI evaluation endpoint: `POST /api/evaluate`
* Health endpoint: `GET /health`

25. VISUAL DESIGN
=================

The entire application must follow a **minimal enterprise design**.

Primary visual characteristics:

* Clean
* Simple
* Professional
* Neutral
* Functional
* Easy to scan
* Low visual noise

Color palette:

* Light gray / white background
* Dark gray text
* Neutral gray borders
* One restrained blue accent

Status colors ONLY:

* Red = critical
* Orange = warning
* Green = success/online
* Blue = information

Do NOT use:

* gradients
* neon colors
* purple AI theme
* glowing effects
* glassmorphism
* 3D effects
* futuristic decorations
* excessive shadows
* colorful backgrounds
* oversized cards
* excessive rounded corners

Cards should use:

* consistent spacing

Do not make every piece of information a separate card.

Prefer:

* tables
* compact sections
* simple rows
* clear typography
